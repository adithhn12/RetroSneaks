👟 About RetroSneaks

RetroSneaks is an e-commerce footwear brand focused on delivering trendy and high-quality sneakers at affordable prices. The business operates by sourcing footwear in bulk from different locations, manually inspecting each pair, and selecting only the best products for resale.

Our collections include popular sneaker models such as Travis Scott editions, Air Jordans, Adidas, and other trending designs. RetroSneaks primarily caters to students, sneaker enthusiasts, and budget-friendly buyers who want stylish footwear without paying showroom prices.

The goal of RetroSneaks is to build customer trust by offering quality-checked sneakers, transparent pricing, and a simple shopping experience.

Business Model: Bulk Purchase → Quality Check → Affordable Resale
